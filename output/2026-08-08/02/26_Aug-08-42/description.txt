A dangerous heat wave is baking the Western U.S. this weekend, bringing scorching, record-breaking temperatures and heightened wildfire risks from Arizona to Montana. A powerful high-pressure ridge is trapping the intense heat close to the ground, with forecasts reaching a blistering 115 degrees in Las Vegas, 116 in Phoenix, 118 in El Centro, California, and a record-shattering 104 in Great Falls, Montana. Officials are urging residents to stay indoors and remain cautious as emergency warnings stay in place. Fortunately, meteorologists report that this extreme weather event will be relatively short-lived. Stay cool and safe out there.
