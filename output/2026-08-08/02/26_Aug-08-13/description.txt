Hungary is facing a critical energy crisis as Prime Minister Peter Magyar warns that the country's sole nuclear power plant may be forced to shut down as early as this weekend. The drastic measure is being driven by the rapidly dropping water levels of the Danube River, which is essential for the plant's operations.
