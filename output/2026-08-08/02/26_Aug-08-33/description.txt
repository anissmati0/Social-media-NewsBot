A dangerous heat wave is baking the Western U.S. this weekend, bringing extreme temperatures and heightened wildfire risks from Arizona to Montana. A powerful high-pressure ridge is trapping the heat near the ground, with meteorologists forecasting scorching highs of 115 degrees in Las Vegas, 116 in Phoenix, and record-breaking temperatures reaching 118 in El Centro, California, and 104 in Great Falls, Montana. Residents are being urged to stay indoors to beat the extreme conditions, though forecasters say this intense heat dome will thankfully be short-lived. Stay safe, stay cool, and keep an eye out for local fire alerts.
